Selva Vins

Indigenous grapes, made with respect and honesty.

Selva · Serra de Tramuntana 39°45′ N · 2°54′ E

01Selva

A tiny winery in the village of Selva, at the foot of the Tramuntana. Sincere wines with their own character, from the island’s native grapes: many of them old, some of them nearly lost.

Between the plain of Inca and the mountains, on the road that climbs to Lluc.

02The family

Twenty years in other people’s vineyards. Then one of his own.

Carlos Rodríguez Furthmann spent some twenty years working the island’s vineyards and cellars for others. Selva Vins is where he finally makes wine his own way: with complete freedom, touching it as little as possible, so that every wine can be more authentic, and more itself.

03How it’s made

Solo uva, nada más.Just grapes, nothing else. It says so on the label.

Not in the bottle

  • Commercial yeast
  • Acid corrections
  • Enzymes
  • Nutrients
  • Fining
  • Filtering
  • Pumps

In the bottle

  • Native Mallorcan grapes
  • Their own spontaneous ferment
  • Time
  • A pinch of sulphur, at most*

*Only a very low dose, so the wine reaches you in good shape. Plenty of bottles get none at all.

By hand

02

Bunch by bunch

Sorted at the winery: out go leaves, little stones and any grape that isn’t in good shape.

03

It starts on its own

Spontaneous fermentation. No added yeast, no corrections.

04

Steel, clay or chestnut

Stainless steel, Mallorcan clay or chestnut wood: whatever the wine asks for.

05

Time on the lees

Months on the fine lees, racked by hand, then racked again.

06

By gravity

Bottled by gravity, without pumps. Unfiltered. Capped and labelled by hand.

        06The grapes

        I

        Before phylloxera, Gargollassa was the most planted red grape in Es Raiger, the plain at the foot of these mountains. Its wines won prizes abroad.

        II

        After the plague it slowly disappeared, until it was considered extinct.

        III

        Three vines.

        Then three plants turned up in a vineyard. From those three, the variety came back.

        IV

        Today Gargollassa is the emblem of Selva Vins. It goes into Gargo.
